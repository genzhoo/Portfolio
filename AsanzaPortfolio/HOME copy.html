About Spider-Man

Spider-Man is one of Marvel's most beloved superheroes. Created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko, Peter Parker uses his extraordinary powers to protect New York City while balancing everyday life. He's also known as "The Friendly-neighbourhood Spider-Man". Across live-action Spider-Man has inspired generations with the message: "With Great Power, Comes Great Responsibility"
